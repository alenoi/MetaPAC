# MetaPAC literature watch — 2026-09-02

## Summary

This run added 3 new strongly relevant publications and 7 new search terms after DOI/arXiv/title deduplication against the canonical collection.

## New publications

### REP-LIE — Resource-Efficient Pruning for Transformer via Low-Rank Importance Estimation

Peng Liu, Huibing Zeng, Yiqun Zhang, Yang Yi, Jigang Wu. arXiv:2608.24973, 2026-08-25. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24973

Relevance: REP-LIE estimates pruning importance from gradients of LoRA low-rank matrices instead of full-model gradients and introduces a stability score before iterative pruning. It is directly relevant to MetaPAC's importance-estimation component because it provides a lower-cost alternative signal derived from fine-tuning dynamics.

### Q-Strata — Hierarchical Bit Allocation for Mixed-Precision Quantization of Mixture-of-Experts LLMs

Deokjae Lee, Sihun Chu, Hyun Oh Song. arXiv:2608.30564, 2026-08-31. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30564

Relevance: Q-Strata constructs per-block Pareto frontiers and optimizes a model-level objective over block budgets, explicitly modeling inter-block coupling. It is a strong adaptive allocation baseline for MetaPAC, particularly for future MoE experiments and for separating importance estimation from action allocation.

### COEC — Calibrated Orthogonal-Equivalence Compensation for Structured Pruning of Large Language Models

Peiqi Yu, Nam Ling, Wei Wang, Wei Jiang. arXiv:2608.21142, 2026-08-21. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21142

Relevance: COEC is a training-free post-pruning compensation method based on calibrated alternating orthogonal rotations and singular-value rescaling. It is relevant to MetaPAC's recovery stage as a non-distillation recovery baseline that can be applied independently of the pruning criterion.

## Graph-expansion notes

The REP-LIE branch strengthens the importance-estimation graph around gradient-derived proxies, low-rank adaptation, and stability of importance estimates. Q-Strata extends the adaptive allocation graph toward hierarchical and model-level optimization rather than local additive proxies. COEC expands the recovery graph toward calibration-only compensation methods that do not require fine-tuning or distillation.

## Baseline / competitor assessment

Q-Strata is the strongest new direct allocation baseline in this run. REP-LIE is a direct importance-estimation comparator. COEC is best treated as a recovery ablation/baseline rather than a full MetaPAC competitor.

## Excluded near-matches

SemKV was reviewed but not added because it targets KV-cache quantization rather than model-parameter compression; it remains a useful peripheral reference for importance-aware mixed precision but did not meet the current strong-relevance threshold. Context-compression and RAG-compression papers were also excluded because they compress prompts or retrieved context rather than transformer parameters.
