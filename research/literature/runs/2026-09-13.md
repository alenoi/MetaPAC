## Added publications

1. **GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers** — Elias Frantar, Saleh Ashkboos, Torsten Hoefler, Dan Alistarh; ICLR 2023; arXiv:2210.17323; first submitted 2022-10-31. Approximate-second-order one-shot weight quantization is a foundational deterministic comparator for MetaPAC's importance estimator and low-bit allocator.
2. **SmoothQuant: Accurate and Efficient Post-Training Quantization for Large Language Models** — Guangxuan Xiao et al.; ICML 2023; arXiv:2211.10438; first submitted 2022-11-18. Its equivalent activation-to-weight difficulty migration provides a hardware-friendly W8A8 baseline.
3. **SpQR: A Sparse-Quantized Representation for Near-Lossless LLM Weight Compression** — Tim Dettmers et al.; extended arXiv preprint; arXiv:2306.03078; submitted 2023-06-05. Its high-precision isolation of quantization-error outliers is a direct mixed-representation baseline.
4. **OmniQuant: Omnidirectionally Calibrated Quantization for Large Language Models** — Wenqi Shao et al.; ICLR 2024; arXiv:2308.13137; first submitted 2023-08-25. Learnable clipping and equivalent transformations are calibration-based adaptive controls relevant to MetaPAC.

All four relevance assessments are based on abstracts and primary indexed metadata; full-text assessment is pending.

## New directions and baseline implications

The strongest recovered direction is **where adaptation enters PTQ**: curvature-aware reconstruction (GPTQ), equivalent difficulty migration (SmoothQuant), selective high-precision protection (SpQR), or learned calibration controls (OmniQuant). GPTQ and OmniQuant are strong importance/reconstruction baselines; SpQR is the closest direct mixed-precision representation baseline; SmoothQuant is the strongest hardware-friendly weight-and-activation reference in this batch.
