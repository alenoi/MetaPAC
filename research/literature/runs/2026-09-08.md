## New publications

1. **BeaconKV: Key-Value Cache Compression Guided by Beacon Queries for Efficient Large Reasoning Model Inference** — Janghyeon Kim, Minsoo Kim, Kyuhong Shim, Jungwook Choi. ICML 2026; arXiv:2609.04971, submitted 2026-09-04. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04971  
   Beacon queries act as compact representatives of global query clusters for predicting which KV pairs will be revisited. This is a strong adjacent baseline for extending MetaPAC-style importance estimation and adaptive allocation from weights to runtime state.

2. **Training-Free Halving of Activated Experts in Fine-Grained Mixture-of-Experts Models** — Xing Chen, Hengshuai Yao. arXiv:2609.04575, submitted 2026-09-04. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04575  
   Separates the number of activated experts from the probability mass used for router normalization, enabling a training-free reduction in routed compute. It suggests an additional MetaPAC action dimension for MoE architectures.

3. **When Load-Balancing Goes Too Far: Expert Pruning in Over-Dispersed Mixture-of-Experts Models** — Berkcan Kapusuzoglu et al. arXiv:2609.04453, submitted 2026-09-03. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04453  
   Identifies a regime where router probabilities cease to be reliable importance signals and introduces domain-aware minimax expert-score allocation. This is a direct baseline for MetaPAC importance estimation and constrained action allocation on MoE models.

4. **EoRA: Fine-tuning-free Compensation for Compressed LLM with Eigenspace Low-Rank Approximation** — Shih-Yang Liu et al. ICLR 2026 Workshops; arXiv:2410.21271. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21271  
   Uses activation eigenspaces to prioritize low-rank compensation of compression error without gradient training. It is a strong alternative to MetaPAC's distillation-based recovery after pruning, quantization, or their combination.

5. **BitDistiller: Unleashing the Potential of Sub-4-Bit LLMs via Self-Distillation** — DaYou Du et al. ACL 2024, pp. 102–116. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2024.acl-long.7. https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.7/  
   Combines quantization-aware training and confidence-aware self-distillation for sub-4-bit LLMs. It is a direct baseline for MetaPAC's recovery objective under aggressive quantization.

6. **L4Q: Parameter Efficient Quantization-Aware Fine-Tuning on Large Language Models** — Hyesung Jeon, Yulhwa Kim, Jae-Joon Kim. ACL 2025, pp. 2002–2024. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.99. https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.99/  
   Jointly integrates LoRA and quantization-aware training in a memory-efficient workflow. It provides a peer-reviewed recovery-aware quantization baseline complementary to MetaPAC's distillation path.

7. **OWQ: Outlier-Aware Weight Quantization for Efficient Fine-Tuning and Inference of Large Language Models** — Changhun Lee et al. AAAI 2024, 38(12), 13355–13364; published 2024-03-24. DOI: 10.1609/aaai.v38i12.29237. https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/29237  
   Selectively keeps vulnerable structured weight columns in high precision while quantizing the rest. This is a direct sensitivity-based mixed-precision baseline for MetaPAC.

## Main directions and baseline assessment

The strongest new direction is **dynamic-state and routing compression**: importance estimation is moving beyond static parameter groups toward KV-cache entries and activated experts. BeaconKV, MESA, and the training-free expert-halving method are directly useful for deciding whether MetaPAC should expand its action space to cache retention and MoE routing.

For recovery, EoRA, BitDistiller, and L4Q create a clean three-way comparison among training-free low-rank compensation, distillation-based QAT, and LoRA-integrated QAT. OWQ remains a particularly useful deterministic baseline for separating the value of MetaPAC's learned importance predictor from the downstream precision allocator.
