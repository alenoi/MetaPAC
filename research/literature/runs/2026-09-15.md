## Added publications

1. **SAS: Simple Attention Sparsification via End-to-End Optimization of Context Ranking** — Zhiwei Li et al.; arXiv:2609.13141; submitted 2026-09-11. Trains continuous selector scores directly through the language-modeling objective under a fixed attention budget and integrates the method into a Triton kernel.
2. **APQF: Agentic Profiling-Guided Structured Pruning and Mixed-Precision Quantization with Adaptive Fine-Tuning** — Sadegh Jafari et al.; arXiv:2608.05499; submitted 2026-08-06. Profiling signals guide per-layer pruning ratios, bit widths, and recovery choices proposed by LLM planners.
3. **FlexMoE: One-for-All Nested Intra-Expert Pruning for MoE Language Models** — Fan Mo et al.; arXiv:2606.27866; submitted 2026-06-26. Learns discrete intra-expert pruning actions and exports nested subnetworks across budgets from one training run.

All three relevance assessments are based on abstracts and primary indexed metadata; full-text assessment is pending.

## New directions and baseline implications

The strongest new direction is **learned decision-making over variable compression budgets**. APQF is the closest end-to-end mixed-compression competitor in this batch, replacing MetaPAC's meta-learned importance/action mechanism with profiling plus an LLM planner. FlexMoE is a direct learned-action baseline for multi-budget MoE deployment. SAS extends the same importance-estimation and constrained-allocation pattern from model parameters to runtime attention context.
