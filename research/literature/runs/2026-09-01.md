## Added publications

### Quantization-Aware Healing: A Practical Recipe for Recovering Compressed, 4-Bit LLMs
Bakbergen Ryskulov, Iker García-Ferrero, David Montero, David Jansen, Ali Hashemi, Jezabel R. Garcia, Antonio Tiene, Román Orús. arXiv:2608.20953, 21 August 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20953

Relevance: direct recovery comparison for MetaPAC. The method distills a structurally compressed and 4-bit quantized student from the original uncompressed model and reports substantially faster and more stable recovery than matched quantization-aware training. This makes the choice of teacher checkpoint and recovery objective a concrete MetaPAC ablation target.

### CLEAR: Water-Filling Rank Allocation with Sparse Dictionary Representations for Training-Free LLM Compression
Jingjiang Wei, Ook Lee. Electronics 15(15), 3395, 1 August 2026. DOI: 10.3390/electronics15153395. https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics15153395

Relevance: strong analogue for MetaPAC's adaptive action allocator. CLEAR performs globally coordinated cross-layer budget allocation using activation statistics and a water-filling/KKT solution, rather than assigning layer budgets independently. Its explicit budget guarantee and allocation ablation make it a useful comparison for the separation between importance estimation and action allocation.

### AMQ: Enabling AutoML for Mixed-precision Weight-Only Quantization of Large Language Models
Sangjun Lee, Seung-taek Woo, Jun-gyu Jin, Changhun Lee, Eunhyeok Park. EMNLP 2025, pp. 35532–35550. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1799. https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1799/

Relevance: direct mixed-precision allocation baseline that was missing from the collection. AMQ combines search-space pruning, a quantization proxy, a learned quality predictor, and iterative search to select layer-wise bit-widths under memory constraints. The quality-predictor-plus-allocator decomposition is especially close to the predictor/action split in MetaPAC.

## Graph expansion notes

The recovery branch was expanded from MetaPAC, MiniTRON, QEFT and other recovery-aware compression papers toward quantization-aware healing. The adaptive allocation branch was expanded from GAMMA, MixQuant, SNIPER and KVTuner toward globally constrained water-filling allocation and AutoML-based bit-width search. These branches reinforce a useful decomposition for future MetaPAC comparisons: importance/sensitivity estimation, budget-constrained action allocation, compression execution, and post-compression recovery.

## Priority for MetaPAC

Highest priority: QAH for recovery-stage ablation and AMQ for predictor-plus-allocation comparison. CLEAR is high priority for evaluating whether MetaPAC's clustering-based assignment should be compared against an explicit globally budgeted allocator. No new publication found in the immediate post-2026-08-31 window was stronger than these previously missed works; lower-relevance KV-cache, attention-only, VLM, and interpretability-specific compression papers were intentionally excluded from the canonical collection.
