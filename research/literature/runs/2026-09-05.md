## Added publications

1. **Wei Huang et al. — “SliM-LLM: Salience-Driven Mixed-Precision Quantization for Large Language Models.”** ICML 2025, PMLR 267:25672–25692. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/huang25aa.html
   - Direct baseline for salience-driven group-wise bit allocation and salience-aware quantizer calibration.
2. **Runsheng Bai, Bo Liu, Qiang Liu — “SKIM: Any-bit Quantization Pushing The Limits of Post-Training Quantization.”** ICML 2025, PMLR 267:2520–2534. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/bai25c.html
   - Particularly close to MetaPAC because it couples scaled k-means with greedy channel-wise allocation for arbitrary bit budgets.
3. **Yi Zhang et al. — “Beyond Dynamic Quantization: An Efficient Static Hierarchical Mix-precision Framework for Near-Lossless LLM Compression.”** EMNLP 2025 Industry Track, 2573–2587. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-industry.175.
   - Hierarchical inter-/intra-layer Hessian sensitivity provides a deterministic, hardware-oriented allocation baseline.
4. **Haojie Duanmu et al. — “MxMoE: Mixed-precision Quantization for MoE with Accuracy and Performance Co-Design.”** ICML 2025, PMLR 267:14793–14806. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/duanmu25a.html
   - Combines sensitivity, expert activation dynamics, and hardware resources, then generates mixed-precision GroupGEMM kernels.
5. **Huachen Qi et al. — “Profiling-Free Mixed-Precision Quantization for MoE LLMs via Fuzzy Rule Interpolation.”** ACL 2026, 21484–21499. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.982.
   - Predicts quantization error from sparse samples in a feature space containing bit-width, activation variance, and parameter scale.
6. **Jianing Deng et al. — “GEMQ: Global Expert-Level Mixed-Precision Quantization for MoE LLMs.”** arXiv:2605.23078, 2026-05-21. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23078
   - Uses global linear programming for expert-level allocation and explicitly repairs quantization-induced router shifts.

## Assessment

**Direct or near-direct MetaPAC baselines:** SliM-LLM, SKIM, SHMQ, MxMoE, FRI-MxMoE, and GEMQ. SKIM is the closest new allocator analogue because it also uses k-means structure plus explicit budget allocation. FRI-MxMoE is the closest new predictor analogue because it estimates quantization error from compact layer features. GEMQ and MxMoE materially strengthen the hardware-/system-aware comparison set.

**Metadata-only additions:** 0.

The main new direction is the separation of the compression pipeline into (1) importance or error prediction, (2) constrained allocation, and (3) deployable hardware execution. The new papers provide distinct baselines for all three layers.
