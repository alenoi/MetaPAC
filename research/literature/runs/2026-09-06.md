## Added publications

1. **Utkarsh Saxena, Sayeh Sharify, Kaushik Roy, Xin Wang — “ResQ: Mixed-Precision Quantization of Large Language Models with Low-Rank Residuals.”** ICML 2025, PMLR 267:53095–53114. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/saxena25b.html
   - Uses PCA to isolate a high-variance low-rank activation subspace for higher precision, giving MetaPAC a geometrically derived mixed-action baseline.
2. **Ziyi Guan et al. — “APTQ: Attention-aware Post-Training Mixed-Precision Quantization for Large Language Models.”** DAC 2024. DOI: 10.1145/3649329.3658498.
   - Uses Hessian-trace sensitivity and the nonlinear effect of attention outputs, making it a strong deterministic importance-estimation baseline.
3. **Shiyao Li et al. — “LLM-MQ: Mixed-precision Quantization for Efficient LLM Deployment.”** Efficient Natural Language and Speech Processing Workshop at NeurIPS 2023.
   - Early layer-wise mixed-precision method targeting a specified weight-memory budget; useful for predictor-versus-allocator ablations.
4. **Mariam Rakka et al. — “Mixed-Precision Quantization for Language Models: Techniques and Prospects.”** arXiv:2510.16805, 2025-10-19.
   - A focused taxonomy across weights, activations, and KV caches; valuable as a map and source-discovery hub for the planned MetaPAC systematic review.
