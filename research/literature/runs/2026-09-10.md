## Added papers
- Delta-CoMe: Training-Free Delta-Compression with Mixed-Precision for Large Language Models (2024-06-13; https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08903). Assigns higher precision to singular vectors associated with larger singular values in fine-tuning deltas; a strong deterministic importance-to-bit-allocation baseline for a delta-space extension of MetaPAC.
- BitDelta: Your Fine-Tune May Only Be Worth One Bit (2024-02-15; https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10193). Quantizes fine-tuning deltas to one bit while sharing a high-precision base model, providing a simple low-bit delta-compression baseline and multi-tenant deployment comparison; it is not itself a model-merging algorithm.
- Enhancing Delta Compression in LLMs via SVD-based Quantization Error Minimization (2025-06-05; https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11087). PrinMix derives SVD-space quantization error, uses 0/1 integer linear programming for bit-budget allocation, and corrects sequential quantization reconstruction targets; a close allocator and error-estimation baseline for MetaPAC in delta space. Earlier versions were titled ADAMIX: Adaptive Mixed-Precision Delta-Compression with Quantization Error Optimization for Large Language Models; latest revision checked is 2026-02-15 (v3), not a separate publication.

## Interpretation
Main new direction: compression of fine-tuning deltas through spectral importance and budget-constrained bit allocation, connecting compression and the newly added model-merging research branch. PrinMix is the closest allocator/error-estimation comparison; Delta-CoMe is a deterministic spectral-importance baseline; BitDelta is a simple low-bit delta baseline. These are not direct end-to-end MetaPAC equivalents and delta compression should not be conflated with model merging.

Other surfaced candidates (TreeQ, MPTQ-ViT, A Fast Post-Training Pruning Framework for Transformers) were not added in this bounded run; primary-page/venue and relevance screening deferred, not rejected as irrelevant.
